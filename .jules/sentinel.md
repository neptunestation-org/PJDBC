## 2026-05-15 - [Bypass of JDBC Proxy Drivers via Unimplemented ResultSet Getters]
**Vulnerability:** Security proxy drivers (like `DataMaskingDriver` and `ReadonlyDriver`) that rely on `AbstractResultSet` or the `transformValue` hook can be bypassed by using JDBC 4.1+ methods (e.g., `getObject(int, Class<T>)`) or specialized getters (e.g., `getClob`, `getBlob`) that are not explicitly overridden or routed through the transformation logic.
**Learning:** `AbstractResultSet` in PJDBC provides a `transformValue` hook that is only called by a few standard getters (`getString`, `getObject`, `getInt`, `getBoolean`, etc.). Many other getters, including those for LOBs and the generic `getObject` with a `Class` or `Map` parameter, delegate directly to the underlying driver, completely bypassing the proxy's security logic.
**Prevention:** Security-sensitive drivers MUST explicitly override every data retrieval method in `ResultSet` (and its subclasses like `MaskingResultSet`) to ensure consistent policy enforcement. For complex types that cannot be easily masked or validated, the "fail-secure" approach of throwing a `SQLException` is preferred.
